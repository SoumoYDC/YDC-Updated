Mortgage Calculator

What is a Mortgage Calculator?

A mortgage calculator estimates your fixed monthly principal-and-interest payment for a home loan, based on the loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan term.
This calculator estimates your monthly payment.

Disclaimer : Enter numbers in your local currency — the calculation works the same regardless of currency. This estimate excludes property tax, insurance, and other fees.

Formula

Monthly Payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n = total number of monthly payments (years × 12).

Example

If Loan Amount = 300,000, Interest Rate = 6.5%, and Term = 30 years:

Monthly Payment ≈ 1,896.20

Over 360 monthly payments, total interest paid comes to roughly 382,632 — more than the original loan amount — which shows why interest rate and loan term matter as much as the loan amount itself.
